## How the art is made

- **Rendering:** Blender 5.2.3 from blender.org (Ubuntu's 5.0.1 has no OpenImageDenoise), Cycles on CPU, denoised,
  AgX Medium High Contrast. A 1080p pass takes about 205 s.
- **Textures:** all generated by our scripts. No file from the Minecraft jar is used.
- **Overworld, Nether portal and End:** hand-built voxel scenes.
- **Real structures:** bastion (Bridge), fortress, stronghold portal room, the RSG island, the FSG ruined portal,
  the SSG beached shipwreck and the Ranked plains village come from seed 2026. That world was generated on the official 1.16.1 dedicated server;
  Piotr accepted the EULA. `art-src/mc/` drives the server, reads the region files and extracts the block layout,
  which we rebuild with our textures.
- **Draftout:** a 26.3 cherry grove from the same seed, on Java 25.
- **Ranked:** a plains village at golden hour (`scene_village.py`; `mc/find_village.py` lists the villages near
  spawn with their pieces). One cave mouth by the pond is filled in. The earlier tier-block scene is
  `scene_ranked.py`.
- **No Mojang files** (jar, NBT, region files, extracts) are in this repo. The scripts expect them under `/tmp/srl`.
