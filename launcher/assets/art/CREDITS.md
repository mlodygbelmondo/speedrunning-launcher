# Art, fonts and icons

NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT.

Everything in `launcher/assets/` was copied from the prototype (`bakeoff/assets/`, scene v3). Paths
below are relative to the repository root. These are the developer notes; Settings > Credits shows
`launcher/assets/credits.md` (and `credits.pl.md`), which must stay in line with them.

## Renders (`art/hero/`, `art/card/`)

Every image in `hero/` and `card/` is our own render: Blender 5.2.3, Cycles, OpenImageDenoise and AgX view transform.

- **Scenes:** voxel scenes built in code.
- **Textures:** our own procedural 16×16 textures, generated with numpy/PIL. No file from the Minecraft jar is used.
- **Structures:** where a scene shows a real structure (bastion, fortress, stronghold, mode scenes), its block layout
  was read from a world we generated ourselves with the official dedicated server. It is then rebuilt with our own
  textures. Ranked (`hero/ranked.jpg`, `card/ranked.jpg`) is the plains village that starts at (−816, 32) in Java
  1.16.1, seed 2026, seen from (−870, 84, 95); one cave mouth by its pond is filled in.
- **Sources:** everything lives in `design-proposals/2026-10-25-redesign/`. The scripts are in `art-src/` and the
  1920×1080 masters in `art/masters/`. Its README lists the seed and coordinates of every structure.
- **Sizes:** `bakeoff/tools/make-art.sh design-proposals/2026-10-25-redesign/art/masters` writes the two shipped
  sizes (hero 1280×800, card 640×360) into `bakeoff/assets/art/`; copy them here after a change.

`art/shade-hero.png` is the Home hero shading (1280×800, RGBA): our own CSS gradients
(`design-proposals/2026-10-25-redesign/shade-only.html`) rendered to a transparent PNG, so both prototype builds
and this app draw the same gradient (`bakeoff/REDESIGN.md`).

## Demo avatars (`demo/avatars/`)

Pixel-art avatars generated by `bakeoff/tools/gen_assets.py` (8×8 random blocks, scaled to 64×64). Used only with
`SRL_DEMO=1`.
